\section{Objective}
\label{sec:Objective}
The goal of the experiment is to verify the Rutherford model of the atom.
In addition, the Rutherford scattering formula is tested by determen the differential cross section of $\alpha$-particles scattered by a thin gold foil.
Furthermore, the thickness of the gold foil is determined from the energy loss of the $\alpha$-particles in the foil.
